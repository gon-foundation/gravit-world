We promised to report on this quarter before the end of December. In short, on 27 November 2026:

**Done.** Gravit Verifier 0.7.0 (Apache-2.0), with the fixes from an external review. Its Python and Rust implementations decided 200,000,000 generated cases identically, and a re-run on a second machine gave the same result. The Graviteron, a unit for the weight of evidence, was published as an IETF Internet-Draft, together with GEVP revisions 08 to 12. Eight open proofs of concept and public checks are published, from AI-agent payments in stablecoins to checks of public ledgers.

**Not done.** The mainnet announced for Q4 2026 has a new date, 12 December 2026; the root key ceremony with independent key holders comes first. Card payment is not live yet; TON payment moved to 10 December 2026.

We prefer to state this openly rather than move dates quietly.

We promised to report on this quarter before the end of December. This is the status on 27 November; we will add the last weeks in a short update after 31 December.

## Done

**Gravit Verifier 0.7.0.** Apache-2.0. The release applies the findings of an external review of 0.6.0: one integrity hash per Verified Fact, excluded records never count, the decision id computed over RFC 8785. 54 test vectors at release; the Python reference and the Rust port agree on all of them.
https://github.com/gon-foundation/verifier/releases/tag/v0.7.0

**200 million cases, then a re-run.** In two public runs the two implementations decided 100,000,000 cases each, identically, the second run mostly with malformed and adversarial input. On 14 November we repeated 10,000,000 of them on a second machine at another provider, with newer Python and Rust: all identical. This shows the results do not depend on our machine. It is not an independent audit.
https://github.com/gon-foundation/verifier/tree/main/reports

**A unit for evidence: the Graviteron.** The question we set ourselves was whether a verifiable unit for "how much evidence stands behind a claim" can be defined at all. Our answer, published as draft-gravit-graviteron-00 on 1 November: yes, as the weight of evidence of Turing and Good, in decibans. Zero means no evidence either way; independent operators add; all keys of one operator count once; old checks lose weight unless renewed; a check without a verdict counts nothing and is noted; a reading is always an interval, never a bare number; any signed contradiction stays visible. Each of these 8 properties has a test, and 21 language-neutral test vectors let any other implementation check itself. The draft defines no decision rule. It is experimental, and the measured reliability of real independent operators is the open part.
https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-gravit-graviteron/

**GEVP 08 to 12.** The protocol now builds on SCITT (RFC 9943) and COSE Receipts (RFC 9942). Revision 08 is a correction: it replaced calibration numbers of ours that could not be reproduced, and the old numbers remain documented. Revision 12 names a known limit openly: provenance is self-reported, so a relayed source can look like a second independent one. Individual Internet-Drafts, not IETF standards.
https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-gravit-gevp/

**A hosted verifier.** It issues signed, tamper-evident receipts and keeps a public log. A receipt shows that a decision was not changed later; it does not show that a claim is true.
https://gravitverifier.space/

## Our proofs of concept

Open proofs of concept built by our team on the Gravit Verifier 0.7.0, with synthetic or public data. A company named in a repository has not endorsed it; a proof of concept is not a partnership.

- **AllUnity stablecoins (CHFAU, EURAU, SEKAU).** A gate for AI-agent payments and a check of every mint against MiCA reserve rules: 7 payment scenarios (2 settled, 5 stopped with their reason), 8 reserve scenarios (1 mint allowed, 7 stopped). 8 of 8 tests pass. https://github.com/gon-foundation/gravit-allunity-poc
- **Sygnum.** A gate for AI-agent transfer proposals before the human four-eyes signature: custody, KYC of both clients, balance, limits; from CHF 100,000 three independent operators. https://github.com/gon-foundation/gravit-sygnum-poc
- **DWF Labs, AI loan desk.** A real open model decided 150 synthetic loan requests and approved 53 risky ones. With the gate, 0 of 53 were executed, at about 2.5 ms per decision; in observer mode all 53 are found and signed into the log. An earlier text in this repository described plans as facts; it is marked as such and kept. https://github.com/gon-foundation/gravit-dwf-poc
- **EasyMM.** Observer mode for AI market-making decisions, outside the risk path. https://github.com/gon-foundation/gravit-easymm-poc
- **SAFR (Singapore).** A reference adapter for SAFR, the runtime safeguards for agentic finance developed by the industry under MAS's BuildFin.ai initiative: 10 scenarios, all four SAFR outcomes, an audit log verifiable offline. https://github.com/gon-foundation/gravit-safr-adapter
- **Coffer C-01 (Solana).** A read-only check that every range-manager transition stayed within the policy in force, from the Solana ledger only. Not a security audit. https://github.com/gon-foundation/gravit-coffer-verification
- **TON TN-01.** From the public ledger: 179 early-miner wallets holding 1,081,391,803 TON (GRAM) are suspended until 21 February 2027, with no outgoing transaction since the vote. Not an audit. https://github.com/gon-foundation/gravit-ton-verification
- **Chainlink CL-01.** Every ETH/USD and BTC/USD update from 20 to 27 October 2026: 418 of 418 confirmed. Not an audit of Chainlink. https://github.com/gon-foundation/gravit-chainlink-verification

## Not done

- **Card payment**, planned for the end of November 2026, is not live yet. **TON payment** moved to 10 December 2026.

We prefer to state this openly rather than move dates quietly.

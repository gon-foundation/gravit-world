# First record, 9 November 2026

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the SHA-256 of the files that recorded the history of Gravit Education on this day: the historical register (RU and EN), the manifesto (RU, EN, DE), an internal summary and plan, and the Internet Archive snapshot of decipial.org of 19 November 2019 (https://web.archive.org/web/20191119181306/https://decipial.org/), which shows the six principles.

`SHA256SUMS.txt.ots` is an OpenTimestamps proof for `SHA256SUMS.txt` (sha256 7faf3b13c5457c1b4524b17577f97c44cecf56888e1330ad87cc43b297d024e7), submitted to two independent calendars. Once anchored in Bitcoin it shows that these files existed no later than that block.

What this proves and what it does not: the timestamp proves existence of these exact files at that time. It does not by itself prove the dates of earlier conversations; those need their own sources (see REGISTER.md, rule of the register).
